Create a professional, polished, playable 3D tactical sniper game based on the following concept.

GAME CONCEPT:
The game takes place in a long outdoor battlefield divided by a wide river. Two opposing concrete defensive walls are positioned on opposite sides of the river, approximately 100 meters apart. Each wall represents a team’s defensive position.

The player is positioned behind one wall together with one AI-controlled friendly robot teammate. On the opposite side, there are two enemy AI-controlled robots. The player and all AI characters are equipped with sniper rifles.

CORE GAMEPLAY:
The game should be a turn-based tactical sniper combat experience.

Each side gets a limited firing opportunity during its turn. The player’s team and the enemy team alternate turns.

During the enemy turn, the two enemy snipers hide behind their defensive wall. The player cannot directly see their bodies through the wall.

However, enemy movement should create a tactical visual clue:
- If an enemy moves behind the wall, a subtle green silhouette/shadow should become visible on the surface of the wall.
- The silhouette should accurately indicate the approximate position and movement of the enemy behind the wall.
- If the enemy remains completely still, no silhouette should be visible.
- The player must observe these movement clues and predict the enemy’s position before firing.

DESTRUCTIBLE WALL MECHANIC:
The defensive walls should be partially destructible.

When the player fires:
- The bullet should physically impact the wall.
- A small section of concrete should break or become damaged at the impact point.
- Visible bullet holes, cracks, concrete fragments and dust should appear.
- Repeated shots should gradually create more damage.
- Wall destruction should remain visually consistent with the actual bullet impacts.
- The wall should provide tactical cover, but it should not be completely indestructible.

ENEMY DAMAGE SYSTEM:
Every character should have 200 total HP.

Body damage should be location-based.

Examples:
- Headshot: instant elimination.
- Body/limb hit: approximately 150 HP damage.
- A second successful damaging hit can eliminate the character once HP reaches zero.
- The UI should clearly display remaining HP.
- Characters should react naturally when hit.
- When HP reaches zero, the character should be knocked out/eliminated with an appropriate animation.

The damage system should feel tactical rather than arcade-like.

ENEMY AIM WARNING SYSTEM:
When enemies are preparing to fire at the player, the player should receive a visual warning.

A red laser-like targeting line should appear on the player's side of the battlefield, showing the approximate direction/target area of the enemy sniper.

The red targeting indicator should:
- Move as the enemy adjusts their aim.
- Give the player a short reaction window.
- Encourage the player to move or reposition behind cover.
- Disappear or resolve when the enemy fires.

The player must use movement and positioning to avoid incoming sniper fire.

ENEMY FIRING MECHANIC:
Each enemy should have one firing opportunity during their turn.

If an enemy fires:
- If the shot hits the player, apply appropriate damage.
- If the shot misses, the enemy loses that firing opportunity.
- A missed shot should create an impact effect on the environment.
- After the enemy firing phase is completed, control returns to the player's team.

PLAYER TURN:
When the player's turn begins:
- Allow the player to freely inspect the battlefield.
- Move the sniper crosshair precisely.
- Aim through a realistic sniper scope.
- Zoom in and out smoothly.
- Adjust the aim horizontally and vertically.
- Search for enemy movement clues.
- Identify enemy positions using the green wall silhouettes.
- Fire one carefully calculated sniper shot.

The sniper scope should feel realistic and responsive.

The crosshair/aiming system should support smooth precision movement and should not feel clumsy or delayed.

FRIENDLY AI ROBOT:
The player has one AI-controlled robot teammate.

The friendly robot should:
- Have a high-quality 3D character model.
- Carry a realistic sniper rifle.
- Have its own HP.
- Take cover intelligently.
- Observe enemy movement.
- Participate in the team's turn.
- Occasionally fire at enemies according to the tactical situation.
- React to being hit.
- Be eliminated if its HP reaches zero.
- Never behave randomly in a way that breaks the gameplay.

The friendly robot should feel like an actual tactical teammate rather than a decorative NPC.

ENEMY AI:
The enemies should be intelligent tactical sniper robots.

They should:
- Take cover behind the wall.
- Remain still when appropriate.
- Move strategically.
- Occasionally reposition.
- Avoid exposing themselves unnecessarily.
- Aim at the player and friendly robot.
- Generate the red targeting warning before firing.
- Make both accurate and inaccurate shots depending on difficulty.
- Become more challenging as levels progress.

The AI should avoid repetitive patterns so that every round feels different.

LEVEL PROGRESSION:
The game should contain progressively harder levels.

As the player advances:
- Enemy AI becomes smarter.
- Enemy movement becomes less predictable.
- Enemy accuracy gradually increases.
- Enemy reaction time improves.
- Battlefield conditions become more challenging.
- Additional enemy units may appear.
- Enemy positions and movement patterns become increasingly difficult to predict.
- Some levels should contain fewer enemies but highly intelligent enemies.
- Other levels should contain more enemies with different tactical behavior.

Difficulty should scale naturally rather than simply increasing enemy HP.

RANDOMIZATION:
Every level should contain controlled procedural/random variation.

Randomize elements such as:
- Enemy starting positions.
- Enemy movement patterns.
- Which enemy moves first.
- Enemy hiding positions.
- Enemy firing accuracy.
- Environmental conditions.
- Minor battlefield details.
- Enemy behavior patterns.

The game should remain fair and solvable while ensuring that repeated playthroughs do not feel identical.

Use high-quality professional 3D character models.

The player, friendly robot and enemy robots should have:
- Detailed 3D models.
- Proper proportions.

The visual quality should feel like a polished modern tactical game rather than a basic prototype.

Use realistic-looking sniper rifles.

The sniper should include:
- Detailed 3D weapon model.
- Realistic scope.
- Smooth aiming.
- Scope zoom.
- Proper recoil.
- Muzzle flash.
- Bullet trail where appropriate.
- Bullet impact effects.
- Concrete debris when hitting walls.
- Different impact effects for walls and characters.
- Realistic reload behavior where appropriate.

The weapon should feel powerful, precise and satisfying to use.

Create intuitive controls suitable for both mobile and desktop gameplay.

- Touch-based aiming.
- Smooth drag controls.
- Dedicated fire button.
- Scope/zoom control.
- Movement/reposition controls.
- Clear tactical UI.

Desktop:
- Mouse-based aiming.
- Keyboard movement.
- Mouse click firing.
- Smooth camera control.

Controls must be responsive and optimized for precision sniper gameplay.

Avoid excessive UI clutter.

The interface should feel modern, professional and immersive.

GAME FLOW:
1. Start the game.
2. Display a professional tactical introduction/tutorial.
3. Enter Level 1.
4. Player and friendly robot take defensive positions.
5. Enemy robots take positions on the opposite wall.
6. Enemy movement creates green silhouettes only when they move behind cover.
7. Player studies the movement clues.
8. Enemy turn begins and enemies can aim at the player.
9. Red targeting indicators warn the player of incoming sniper aim.
10. Player can reposition to avoid being hit.
11. Enemy fires one shot per available firing opportunity.
12. Player's turn begins.
13. Player aims through the sniper scope.
14. Player fires one tactical shot.
15. Bullet damages the wall and/or enemy depending on impact.
16. HP and character state update immediately.
17. Friendly AI teammate participates in the tactical exchange.
18. Turns continue until one team is eliminated.
19. Winning the round unlocks the next level.
20. Difficulty increases progressively.

WIN/LOSS CONDITIONS:
The player's team wins when all enemy units are eliminated.

The player loses when the player and friendly teammate are both eliminated.

Provide clear but professional victory and defeat screens with options to:
- Continue.
- Retry.
- Restart level.
- Return to level selection.

OVERALL EXPERIENCE:
The final result should feel like a premium tactical sniper strategy game focused on observation, prediction, precision aiming, destructible cover, turn-based combat and intelligent AI.

The most important gameplay loop should be:

OBSERVE → DETECT MOVEMENT → PREDICT POSITION → AIM → FIRE → DAMAGE COVER/ENEMY → SURVIVE ENEMY AIM → REPOSITION → REPEAT.

Do not make the game feel like a simple shooting gallery.

Make positioning, timing, observation and precision the core of the experience.

Build the game as a complete playable experience with polished 3D visuals, realistic sniper mechanics, intelligent AI, destructible walls, responsive controls, immersive audio, progressive difficulty and randomized tactical situations.
